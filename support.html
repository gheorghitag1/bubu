Reminders don't arrive

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Bubu and make sure notifications are allowed. In the app, check Settings → Reminders. Reminders stop automatically once you reach your daily goal.

Apple Watch doesn't show my water

Open Bubu on your iPhone once, then on your Watch. Both apps sync automatically when the devices are near each other.

Restore Bubu Premium

Open the app → Settings → About → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bubu Premium → Cancel Subscription.
